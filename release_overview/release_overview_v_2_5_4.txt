DataViewer Enterprise - v2.5.4 - 2026-06-24

Fixes for the pop-out side panels (Notes and Navigator) in TPM mode.

Bug Fixes
- After popping a side panel out into its own window, you can now dock it back
  in - drag it to any edge, or use the panel's dock button. Previously a floated
  panel could not be re-docked. Both the Notes and Navigator panels are fixed.
- The Navigator panel now remembers its position and floating state between
  sessions, just like the Notes panel already did. Whatever arrangement you set
  is restored next time you open the app.
- On the first launch after this update, the Notes and Navigator panels reset to
  an identical default width so the two sides start balanced; after that your own
  sizing and placement is remembered.
