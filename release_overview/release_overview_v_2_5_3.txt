DataViewer Enterprise - v2.5.3 - 2026-06-24

Refinements to the new editable Notes panel (TPM mode), based on look-and-feel feedback.

New Features / Improvements
- The Notes and Navigator side panels can now be popped out and pinned to any
  edge of the window - top, left, right, or bottom - so each user can arrange
  the workspace however feels natural. The two panels behave identically.
- On first launch after this update, the Notes and Navigator panels open at the
  same default width, so the two sides of the window look balanced out of the
  box. Once you resize or move a panel yourself, your arrangement is remembered.
- The measurement details inside each note card (TPM before/after, draw
  pressure, clog, smell, puffing regime) are now left-justified instead of
  centered, giving the cards a cleaner, easier-to-scan layout.
