DataViewer Enterprise - v2.4.3 - 2026-06-16

This is an internal smoke-test build that bundles the v2.4.2 connection
foundation together with a round of live-collaboration improvements. It is
not a deployed release; it exists so the team can exercise the new
reconnect behaviour against a real database and network.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

Reliability

- Live updates now survive a network hiccup. If your connection drops for a
  moment or your Wi-Fi is spotty, the app no longer quietly stops receiving
  updates from your teammates. It notices the interruption, reconnects on its
  own, and picks the live feed back up where it left off.

- Catches "half-dead" connections. On stricter office or VPN networks a
  connection can look alive while silently passing nothing through. The app
  now actively checks that its link to the database is really working and, if
  it isn't, fails over and recovers instead of going silent with no warning.

- Re-subscribes to everything after reconnecting. When the link comes back,
  the app re-registers for every kind of update it was watching before, so no
  category of change gets missed after a blip.

Bug Fixes

- No more lost edits after a reconnect. When the connection is restored, the
  app re-syncs the file you have open. If a teammate changed something while
  you were disconnected AND you had your own unsaved edits, the app now merges
  both - you keep your work and you also see their change. Previously a
  reconnect could quietly drop one side or the other.

- "Who's viewing / editing" comes back. Your presence indicator is
  re-published to the rest of the team after you reconnect, so colleagues can
  once again see that you're in the file instead of you appearing to have left.

New Features

- Automatic cleanup of older score data in the background. Scores that were
  saved in an older internal format are now converted to the current numeric
  format automatically - once when the system first updates, and then quietly
  each night. This happens with no action on your part and keeps everyone's
  data consistent and comparable.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

Also included: the v2.4.2 connection-hardening foundation (a more robust,
self-healing database connection) that the improvements above are built on.
