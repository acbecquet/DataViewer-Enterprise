DataViewer Enterprise - v2.4.14 - 2026-06-23

Bug Fixes
- Loading a Sensory session from the Database Browser that was already open no
  longer creates a duplicate entry. The app now recognizes the already-open
  session by its database record and switches to it, keeping all of the file's
  data in a single record (previously a re-load could split it across two).

Internal
- Continuation of the duplicate-on-reopen fixes (see v2.4.11 - v2.4.13).
