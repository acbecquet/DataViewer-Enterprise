DataViewer Enterprise - v2.8.0 - 2026-07-14

This release bundles the v2.7.x development cycle into one deployable build.
It makes TPM plot notes far more useful and refines the sensory workflow and reports.

New Features

- Annotated plot export. The "Save plot" button on a TPM Trend now exports a
  richer image: every flagged point gets its own note textbox across the top of the
  chart, joined to its data point by a thin arrow that stops just above the point so
  it never crosses the plotted data. Notes on the same or nearby puffs stack neatly
  instead of overlapping, the plot title always stays readable on the top layer, and
  the image grows only as tall as the notes need. In the rare case a note sits right
  against the left edge, its box eases into the plot at half opacity so you can still
  read the chart behind it. On-screen plots and PowerPoint reports are unchanged -
  this applies only to the saved plot image.

- Click a plot point to jump to its note. In TPM mode, clicking a flagged point on
  the trend now switches to that point's sample and highlights the matching note
  card, so you can move from a measurement straight to the observation behind it.
  (This complements the existing note-to-plot link, which rings the point on the
  chart when you click a note.)

- More heating technologies. The sensory heating-technology dropdown now includes
  S17B, S25B, and S25B1, and choosing one auto-fills its typical coil resistance
  (1.3, 1.1, and 1.0 ohm respectively). The value stays editable.

Improvements

- Clearer sensory report radar. The dashed reference rings on the sensory radar
  charts are darker and thicker, so they read cleanly on a projector.

- Roomier report titles. Every report slide title is 2 points smaller, leaving room
  for longer titles without truncation.

Quality

- The full automated test suite (54 suites, including live-database end-to-end
  checks) is green, and the build is warning-clean.
