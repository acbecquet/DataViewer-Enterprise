DataViewer Enterprise - v2.5.12 - 2026-06-25

New Features
- Sensory stopwatch hotkey rebinding now shows clear confirmation. The
  "Stopwatch Hotkey" button in Settings has a keyboard icon, and the rebind
  dialog displays the key you pressed and asks you to confirm with "Set hotkey"
  -- so you can see at a glance that the right key registered before it takes
  effect.

Bug Fixes
- Selecting a sample card now works no matter where you click on it. Previously
  the active-card outline (and the Space stopwatch hotkey) only followed a click
  into a text box; clicking a label or empty part of a card left the hotkey stuck
  on the first sample. Now a click anywhere on a card makes it the active one, so
  Space starts/stops the stopwatch on whichever sample you clicked.

Under the hood (data safety)
- Hardened the Sensory session save path so a sample added to a session from
  another source while you have it open can no longer be silently dropped when you
  save. This is groundwork for the upcoming phone sensory web form.
- Made the Sensory "session name" consistent across saving and the Excel/panelist
  import paths, so re-saving an imported session no longer accidentally splits it
  into a duplicate.
