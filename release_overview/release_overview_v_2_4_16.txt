DataViewer Enterprise - v2.4.16 - 2026-06-23

Improvements
- When you close the app after making changes and choose "Save All", the progress
  bar now appears immediately and stays up for the entire save - including the
  first couple of seconds (writing your edits to the database) that previously
  showed a held, frozen window with no indicator. Saving your work and writing the
  offline copy now read as ONE continuous progress bar from start to finish, so
  it's always clear the app is working.

Internal
- The close -> save -> offline-snapshot sequence now shares a single progress
  dialog (shown from the first step; no flash of a second one). Other long
  operations - loading files, generating reports, refreshing the snapshot, and
  repairing data - already display progress and are unchanged.
