DataViewer Enterprise - v2.4.4 - 2026-06-17

This is an internal smoke-test build. It bundles a round of durability and
resilience work (sub-plan 3) on top of the v2.4.3 live-update and reconnect
improvements. It is not a deployed release; it exists so the team can put the
new behaviour through a real database, a crash/power-loss test, and a few
large-workbook saves before it ships.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

Reliability

- Your offline cache is now crash-safe. The app keeps a local copy of your
  data so you can still read it when the server is unreachable. Previously, a
  crash or power loss while that cache was being refreshed could leave you
  with a half-written, unusable cache - or none at all. The new build builds
  the fresh cache off to the side and swaps it in atomically only once it is
  complete, so an interruption can never leave you worse off than before.

- The app recovers gracefully from locked or encrypted local files. Its
  recovery data and offline cache live in files on your PC. If one of those
  files is temporarily locked by another program, or gets caught by a
  document-protection (encryption) policy, the app no longer silently loses
  that data. It detects the problem, keeps your information safe in the open
  session, and warns you loudly instead of failing in the background.

- The "last synced" time is now accurate. The offline cache's "last synced"
  timestamp is taken from the server's clock rather than your PC's, so it
  stays correct even if your computer's clock is wrong or set to a different
  time zone.

Bug Fixes

- Saving large workbooks no longer freezes the app. On big files, writing
  your edits back to Excel could lock up the window and show "Not Responding"
  until it finished. Those writes now happen in the background, so the app
  stays responsive while it saves, with a sensible time limit that scales to
  the size of the workbook.

- Clearer warnings when something can't be synced or backed up. If a local
  change cannot be queued for sync, or your recovery/offline data cannot be
  written, the app now tells you plainly instead of letting it slip by
  unnoticed - and your edit is kept so it can be written on the next save.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

This build follows on from the v2.4.3 live-update and reconnect work; the two
together round out the multi-user reliability effort.
