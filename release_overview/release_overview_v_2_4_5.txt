DataViewer Enterprise - v2.4.5 - 2026-06-19

New Features

- Database Browser version & health triage. Every tab in the Database Browser
  now shows two new columns: "Version" (which app era a record came from - e.g.
  v2.2.x, or "v2.3.x (approx.)" / "pre-v2.4.2 (unstamped)" for older records saved
  before version stamping) and "Health" (flags records that may need attention:
  "Legacy string scores", "Junk candidate", or "No samples"). A new "Version"
  dropdown beside the search box lets you filter the list to specific eras and
  health states - combined with the text search - so you can quickly find and
  review old or incomplete records across TPM, Sensory, and Detailed Sensory data.

- Cleanup and one-click repair. Delete confirmations now list exactly which
  records will be removed (name and era) so nothing is deleted by surprise.
  "Repair Legacy Scores" losslessly converts old text-stored scores back to numbers
  across the whole database, and "Repair Incomplete..." rebuilds older TPM files
  that were saved without their detailed measurement rows by re-reading the
  original Excel file.

Performance

- Much faster app close. Closing the app no longer rebuilds the entire local
  offline backup every time. It now checks whether anything changed since the last
  backup and skips the rebuild when nothing did, so closing is near-instant in the
  common case (previously this could take roughly ten seconds against the shared
  database). Closes after you have actually changed data still refresh the backup.
