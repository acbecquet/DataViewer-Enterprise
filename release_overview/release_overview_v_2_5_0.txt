DataViewer Enterprise - v2.5.0 - 2026-06-23

This release rolls the v2.4.x reliability, performance, and usability work up into
one deployable version.

Saving & Data Integrity
- Fixed a save regression that could silently drop edits or create false duplicate
  records; saves now retry/verify and never discard your work without telling you.
- Re-opening a session that is already open now switches to it instead of creating
  a duplicate that split its database record. Fixed across every way of opening a
  session: a single file, multiple files, the Database Browser, and crash recovery.

Performance
- Loading files and closing without saving are now effectively instant.
- Closing after edits is much faster: the offline copy is rebuilt incrementally
  (only what changed) instead of re-copying everything on every close.

Clarity & Feedback
- Closing and choosing "Save All" now shows one continuous progress bar from the
  very first step - no more held, frozen window before the bar appears.

Usability
- "Save Test Headers" presets now persist reliably.
- Sensory dropdowns have a per-entry remove (x) control, and the preset dropdown
  now drops below the field and scrolls when the list is long.

Reliability
- Hardened backward compatibility with older data, offline/online transitions, and
  automatic recovery of your last session (open files + unsaved edits) after a crash
  or an unexpected close.
